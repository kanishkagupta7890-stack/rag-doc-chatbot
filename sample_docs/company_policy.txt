Company Policy Handbook - Sample Document

REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Remote work requests must be submitted at least 48 hours in advance through the HR portal.
Employees working remotely must be available during core hours, 10 AM to 4 PM local time.

VACATION POLICY
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid vacation per month, totaling 18 days per year.
Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.
Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for approval.

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Employees can claim reimbursement for business-related travel, meals, and accommodation.
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Receipts are required for any expense over $25.
Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval.

EQUIPMENT POLICY
New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and standard accessories within their first week.
Equipment requests for additional hardware must be approved by the department head.
All company equipment must be returned within 5 business days of an employee's last day.

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December.
Reviews include a self-assessment, manager feedback, and goal setting for the next cycle.
Salary adjustments, if any, are typically communicated within 30 days of the review.
